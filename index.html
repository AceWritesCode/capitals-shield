How it Works

What is Capital's Shield?

Capital's Shield is a dynamic risk management engine. Instead of risking the same fixed amount every trade, the app dynamically sizes your position based on whether you are winning or losing. It aggressively cuts your risk during losing streaks to protect your capital, while compounding your risk with "house money" during winning streaks to build massive profit runs.

The Core Engine

  • Daily Risk Limit: The maximum amount of your own capital you are willing to lose in one day. The app splits this into smaller "bullets" so you never blow it all on one trade.
  • Compound Risk %: When you win a trade, instead of pocketing all the profit, you take a percentage (e.g. 50%) of that profit and add it to your baseline risk for the next trade. This allows you to risk massive amounts without ever touching your base capital.

Example Scenario

Settings: $10,000 Bal | 2% Daily Risk ($200) | 4% Daily Target ($400) | 33% Compound Risk | R:R Goal 2.0

The Setup: You've set aside a hard line of $200 as your maximum loss for the day. If you strictly follow the app's sizing, it is mathematically impossible to lose more than this amount.

Winning Trades: With compound risk at 33%, your first risk will be exactly 33% of your $200 limit (i.e. $66). If you win at a 2.0 RR, you make $132 profit. Your daily available risk pool immediately grows to $332 ($200 base limit + $132 "house money" profit). Your Next Risk is automatically calculated as 33% of this new, larger pool, bumping your risk up to $109 for the second trade. By compounding your wins this aggressively, you can hit your $400 daily target incredibly fast—often in just 2 or 3 trades!

Losing Trades: When you lose, your remaining risk pool decreases. Because the app calculates your next position size based on this shrinking pool, your risk gets smaller with every loss. This extends your survivability drastically, allowing you to endure 5 to 6 consecutive losses before hitting your daily stop.

The Golden Rule: Win big on streaks, survive longer on drawdowns, and never lose more than your set daily limit.
